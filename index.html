Practical Notes

Getting Around Barcelona

Metro + walking covers most Barcelona days. Taxis/ride-hail for airport transfers and late nights. T-Casual card for 10 metro rides.

Meals & Timing

Spanish lunch: 13:30–15:00. Dinner: 20:30–22:00. Restaurants fill at these times. Earlier slots are usually available without a reservation.

Summer Heat

August highs of 30–33°C. Plan indoor activities or rest for 14:00–16:00. Stay hydrated. Sunscreen essential for beach and walking days.

Costa Brava Driving

Roads to coves are narrow and winding. Park early at popular beaches. Bring water, towels, and snorkeling gear in the car.

Connectivity

EU roaming or local eSIM. Download offline maps for Costa Brava — mobile signal can be spotty in coves and villages.

Language

Catalan and Spanish are both spoken. English widely understood in Barcelona and tourist areas. A few Catalan phrases are appreciated.
