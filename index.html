Practical Notes

Getting Around Barcelona

From the W, use taxis for major sightseeing moves rather than metro. Le Méridien is walkable to everything central. T-Casual card for metro if needed.

Meals & Timing

Spanish lunch: 13:30–15:00. Dinner: 20:30–22:00. Restaurants fill at these times. Earlier slots usually available without reservation.

Summer Heat

August highs of 30–33°C. The W pool/beach makes afternoon heat manageable. Sunscreen essential for beach and walking days.

Costa Brava Driving

Roads to coves are narrow and winding. Park early at popular beaches. Bring water, towels, and snorkeling gear in the car.

Connectivity

EU roaming or local eSIM. Download offline maps for Costa Brava — signal can be spotty in coves and villages.

Two Hotels, Two Vibes

W Barcelona = beachfront resort, pool afternoons, waterfront dining. Le Méridien = central city, walk-everywhere, Gothic Quarter at your doorstep.
